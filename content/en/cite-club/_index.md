Our growing collection of social science work on the topic of Language Machines. 

## What is a Cite Club?

Events in the tech world with respect to AI and LLMs are happening at such a pace it is difficult to keep on top of current scholarship. The aim of this project is to increase visibility of recent publications in the social sciences of language machines. Although we are specifically interested in the tools of linguistic anthropology and closely related disciplines (EMCA, sociolinguistics, applied linguistics, media anthropology, sociology, language and technology philosophy, STS), we are happy to include publications from other disciplines (media studies, digital humanities, HRI, CSCW, history of science, computer science, etc.) as long as they further the conversation that brings us to together.

The Cite Club is a community-sourced resource. We send out a call for your contributions periodically over the mailing list.
