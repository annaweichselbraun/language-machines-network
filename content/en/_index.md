**Language Machines Network** is a research network at the intersection of linguistic anthropology, LLMs, science and technology studies (STS), and related fields. We meet monthly online to discuss pre-circulated work — our own and others' — on language and large language models.

Our work includes a [special issue on "Language Machines" in the *Journal of Linguistic Anthropology*](https://anthrosource.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/toc/10.1111/%28ISSN%291548-1395.language-machines), which continues to accept contributions on a rolling basis — see the [Cite Club](/cite-club/) page for our publications and curated readings, and the [Calendar](/calendar/) for our reading group schedule.

[Click here](https://www.upress.umn.edu/9781517919320/language-machines/) for Leif Weatherby's excellent book _Language Machines: Cultural AI and the End of Remainder Humanism_.

## Join us

To join the mailing list, email **[languagemachinesnetwork@gmail.com](mailto:languagemachinesnetwork@gmail.com)**. We add people to the list individually, so tell us a bit about your interest in the network when you write in.
